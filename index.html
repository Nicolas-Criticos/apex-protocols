Program Overview

An ongoing strength program built around the Push / Pull / Legs split. Training progressively overloads week over week, adding reps, weight, or intensity as you adapt. All training is performed with free weights and kettlebells only. Rest days are active recovery: mobility work, breathwork, or a 20-min walk. This is your foundation. Everything that follows is built on it.

Meal Timing
Pre-Workout
Carbs + protein 60-90 min before training. Rice, oats, chicken, egg whites.
Post-Workout
Protein + fast carbs within 45 min. Whey shake, banana, white rice.
Before Bed
Slow protein to fuel overnight recovery. Cottage cheese, casein, Greek yogurt.
Top Protein Sources
  • Chicken breast - lean, versatile
  • Eggs & egg whites
  • Whey protein isolate
  • Lean beef mince (90%+)
  • Tuna & white fish
  • Greek yogurt (plain)
Quality Carb Sources
  • White & brown rice
  • Oats - slow-release energy
  • Sweet potato
  • Banana - pre/post workout
  • Wholegrain bread
  • Fruit - apple, berries, mango
Healthy Fat Sources
  • Avocado
  • Olive oil - cooking & dressing
  • Nuts - almonds, walnuts
  • Salmon & fatty fish
  • Nut butter (natural)
Coach's Nutrition Rules
  • 01Hit your protein target every single day - non-negotiable.
  • 02Drink 3-4L of water. Most cravings are dehydration in disguise.
  • 03Don't fear carbs on training days - they fuel your lifts.
  • 04Reduce carbs on rest days, keep protein the same.
  • 05Sleep 7-9 hours. No supplement beats sleep for recovery.
